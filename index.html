Privacy Policy

Linghe Pet - Last updated: [2026-06-03]

1. Introduction

Linghe Pet ("App") is a desktop virtual pet application powered by on-device large language model, speech recognition (ASR) and text-to-speech (TTS). We prioritize user privacy; all chat content, voice recordings and AI inference data are processed entirely locally on your Android device and never uploaded to our servers or any third-party cloud. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you use our App, in compliance with GDPR (EU/EEA), CCPA (California, USA) and Google Play policies.

2. Data We DO NOT Collect

  • No chat logs, voice audio, pet interaction content is transmitted to external servers. All LLM, ASR and TTS computation runs locally via open-source on-device engine.
  • We never collect your microphone raw data for remote storage; mic input only works temporarily on-device for real-time voice chat and gets discarded immediately after processing.

3. Limited Data We Collect (Only with your consent)

3.1 Firebase Analytics & Crash Report

With your opt-in consent, anonymous crash logs and aggregated anonymous usage statistics are collected via Google Firebase to fix bugs and improve App experience. No personal identifiable information (PII) is included. You may disable analytics collection anytime inside App Settings.

3.2 Advertising Identifier (AAID)

If you agree to personalized ads under GDPR/CCPA popup, AdMob accesses your Android Advertising ID to serve relevant ads. If you reject personalized advertising, only non-personalized contextual ads will be shown.

4. In-App Purchases

All subscription and one-time purchase transactions are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. We cannot access your payment card, bank info or billing details. All payment data is stored securely by Google per Google's privacy rules.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: For in-app advertising, governed by Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase: Analytics & crash tracking, governed by Google Privacy Policy above. We have no control over third-party data practices of Google.

6. User Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

  • Right to access & delete: You can clear all local App data anytime via Android system App info or inside App setting; no user data stored on our server to delete remotely.
  • Opt-out personalized ads: Toggle via initial GDPR popup or App privacy setting.
  • California residents: You may select "Do Not Sell My Data" in privacy setting; we never sell any user information.

7. Local AI Model Files

Large language model, ASR/TTS model files are downloaded on your demand inside App. All model files save locally on your phone storage, no automatic background upload of model or user-generated content. You can delete downloaded model packages at any time from App storage page.
