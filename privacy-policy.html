3. Google AdMob and Advertising

The overseas version of Linghe Pet uses Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service provided by Google LLC ("Google"), to display ads. AdMob is integrated via the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

3.1 Data Collected by Google AdMob

When you use the overseas version of the App, AdMob may collect the following information automatically:

  • Google Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS / GAID on Android)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size, language)
  • App version and session information
  • Ad impression, click, and conversion data
  • Coarse geographic location (country/region level) derived from IP

3.2 Personalized vs Non-Personalized Ads

AdMob may serve either personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your consent:

  • Personalized ads: ads selected based on your interests inferred from your prior app usage, demographics, and location. Google may use your Advertising ID for this purpose.
  • Non-personalized ads: ads selected using contextual information only (e.g., the app's content category), with frequency capping still applied. No interest-based profiling is performed.

You can change your ad personalization preference at any time through the App's Settings → Privacy Options menu, which invokes Google's UMP consent form. You may also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at the device level:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of ad personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

3.3 GDPR Consent (UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App displays a consent dialog on first launch (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform). The dialog clearly presents options to Accept or Reject personalized advertising. You may change your choice at any time via Settings → Privacy Options. We do not hide, pre-check, or otherwise obscure the "Reject" option.

3.4 CCPA Opt-Out Signals

For users in California, the App respects the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal transmitted by your browser or device. If a GPC signal is detected, AdMob will automatically treat your request as a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" request, and we will not share your personal information with AdMob for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Your Rights Under CCPA/CPRA (California)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100, § 1798.110, § 1798.115): request what personal information we collect, the categories of sources, the business or commercial purpose, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete (§ 1798.105): request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct (§ 1798.106): request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information (§ 1798.121).
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing (§ 1798.120, § 1798.135): we do not sell personal information for money. We may "share" identifiers with AdMob for cross-context behavioral advertising; you can opt out via the in-app Privacy Options menu or by enabling Global Privacy Control (GPC) in your browser/device.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination (§ 1798.125): we will not deny service, charge different prices, or provide a different level of quality for exercising your CCPA rights.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: To submit a verified opt-out request, you can:

  1. Open the App → Settings → Privacy Options → Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information, or
  2. Enable Global Privacy Control (GPC) in your browser or device, or
  3. Email us at kone_net@163.com with subject "CCPA Opt-Out".

We will respond to verifiable consumer requests within 45 days. If you authorize an agent to act on your behalf, we will require proof of authorization.
